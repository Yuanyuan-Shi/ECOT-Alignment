Each iteration shuffles all ten distinct tasks without replacement and collects
four independently seeded episodes per task. This gives 40 accepted episodes in
ten task-specific groups of four. Advantages are normalized only inside each
same-task group. The run uses only the fresh batch from the current iteration,
refreshes the frozen rollout policy once at its boundary, and performs one GRPO
update epoch. Ten iterations produce 400 accepted training episodes and ten W&B
points for the reward and `train/grpo_objective` curves.

The task subset is `[4, 17, 23, 28, 41, 65, 70, 72, 84, 86]`; task 84 replaces
the repeatedly slow task 27. Sampling uses `do_sample=true`, temperature 1.0,
top-p 1.0, and no top-k filtering. The optimization configuration remains
lambda 0.8, KL beta 0.04, clip epsilon 0.2, and learning rate 1e-6.

The run retains only the newest policy needed for deterministic resume. Once the
next iteration has durably copied it, the previous intermediate checkpoint is
removed. The final iteration-10 policy is retained. Rollout data, metrics, group
statistics, audits, completion markers, and W&B history remain available.

Expected completion is approximately 03:00–05:00 PDT on 2026-09-12, with 06:00
as a conservative estimate. GNOME's AC sleep timeout is configured as zero, so
