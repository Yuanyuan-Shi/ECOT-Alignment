## Interpretation to preserve

- None of the tuned checkpoints improved task success. Lambda 0.3 was the
  closest tradeoff versus pretrained: +0.026 mean cosine, approximately -0.1
  percentage point pooled Move mismatch, and -2.2 percentage points success.
- Increasing `lambda_move` improved mean cosine and generally reduced Move
  mismatch conditional on successful episodes, but it did not improve overall
  task execution. This is consistent with making actions agree more strongly
  with a generated MOVE claim that is not always task-correct.
- The earlier nine-episode result (24/27 to 25/27 successes) was only a
  one-episode change. Its paired success discordance was two tuned-only wins
  versus one pretrained-only win, so it should be treated as a feasibility
  signal rather than established improvement.
- The training code currently uses the annotation artifact only to select query
  IDs. It does not use the saved `T`/`F` Move label as a training target. It
  parses the original generated MOVE claim and applies
  `L_total = L_performance + lambda_move * (1 - cosine)` to every retained
  query.
- All 1,557 records in the 90-task artifact have source
  `accepted_initial_cosine`; they were not manually corrected. The broad set
  also includes Task 64, whose reversed task plan caused it to be excluded from
  the earlier curated nine-episode review.
- Training exposure differed substantially. The nine-episode run saw roughly
  10.5 effective passes over its 153 training queries and ended at epoch 10.
  The 90-task runs saw roughly 3.2 passes over 1,245 training queries and
  stopped during epoch 4 at 500 steps.
- Validation is split by query within each one-episode task trajectory, so
  adjacent observations from the same episode appear in both train and
  validation. Validation alignment therefore does not measure independent
  closed-loop generalization.

## Suggested next experiment

1. Manually validate MOVE claim correctness, not only claim/action agreement.
2. Gate or weight Move loss using those labels and emphasize genuine
   misalignment examples; do not force actions to follow incorrect reasoning.
3. Exclude invalid plans such as Task 64.
4. Collect multiple training trajectories per task and split validation by
   episode rather than by query.
5. Compare equal data exposure; ten epochs on the 90-task training split is
   approximately 1,560 optimizer steps at the current effective batch size.
6. Increase paired evaluation trials beyond two before drawing a final model
   selection conclusion.
