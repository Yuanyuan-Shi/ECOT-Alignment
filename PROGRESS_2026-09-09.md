## Stop-inclusive rescoring and command/execution gap

User requested stop within 0.03m over the predicted 10-action chunk and explanation
of the policy-versus-realized mismatch gap. Completed read-only rescoring, no training.
New report: analysis_results/move_only_500/stop_execution.html.
Reproduce: evaluate_training_stop_comparison.py (GPU, frozen weights) then
analyze_stop_and_execution.py and render_stop_execution_report.py (scripts/analysis).

CORRECTION: zero translation vectors are NOT all literal stop. Training includes
50 zero vectors but only 13 literal MOVE: stop (1 successful-source, 12 failed-source).
Others include gripper-only, rotation-only, and parse failures/conflicts. Do not
claim those are all stop instructions. New tables include directional + literal stop;
other zero-vector cases remain excluded. No parser or historical metrics were changed.

Threshold provenance: public original ECoT primitive_movements.py uses .03 per-axis
endpoint-state threshold (plus L1 rescaling at .09, rotation/gripper handling), over
up to four states / three transitions. Exact modified LIBERO annotation generator
was not available in the checkpoint repo/dataset. Therefore 10-step stop rule is an
explicit evaluation adaptation, not confirmed identical to original LIBERO labeling.
Installed OSC_POSE maps clipped [-1,1] translation inputs to [-.05,.05] metres.
Stop scored on sum(.05*clip(action_xyz,-1,1)) over all 10 commands. Euclidean and
per-axis .03m checks give identical counts here; both stored. All literal stop
commands fail: 13/13 training each model; test 33/33,53/53,82/82 respectively.
Actual stop endpoint displacements satisfy .03m in 33/33,51/53,82/82 respectively.

Stop-inclusive training success/failure counts:
Original 64/897,37/311; lambda1 19/897,17/311; Move-only 2/897,12/311.
Stop-inclusive test success/failure counts:
Original 148/2228,115/942; lambda1 92/2276,116/695; Move-only 85/2264,117/769.

Gap diagnosis, directional-only: full-command mismatch 230,155,120; restricting to
executed prefixes gives 220,147,116. Clipping/scaling changes none of these counts.
Realized mismatches remain 505,406,475. For Move-only, 393 aligned executed-command
queries become misaligned in realized motion, while 34 reverse the other way.
379/393 lost-alignment cases have realized net displacement <=3cm; 91 have <=3mm
while commanded net exceeds3cm. Median realized vs commanded net in these cases
is 6.96mm vs117.08mm. Gap concentrates in small/stalled actual motions. The finite-
gain OSC torque controller, repeated measured-position-relative goals, and physical
constraints explain why command sums need not equal realized displacement, but
logs lack torque saturation/contact impulses for identifying a unique physical cause.
A simple held-out lag regression FAILED to improve prediction; saved diagnostics
must not be presented as evidence proving lag is the primary cause.
